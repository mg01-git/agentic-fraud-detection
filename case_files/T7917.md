**✅ Mitigating factors:**

**Why this decision:**

The combination of a brand-new funding instrument, a high-risk purchase category, a cross-region shipping/billing mismatch, back-to-back declines, and a newly-created high-risk email attached to an old account together form a strong, coherent stolen-funding-instrument pattern -- this is not a single ambiguous flag but multiple independent signals all pointing the same direction. The known device/location only rules out a device-swap style takeover; it does not offset the fresh-instrument-plus-fraud-pattern signals, which are the dominant risk here. Given the transaction is already declined and the evidence is largely one-sided toward fraud rather than genuinely mixed, reject is warranted over escalate.
